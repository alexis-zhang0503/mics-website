About
the Group

Exploring multimodal sensing and intelligent communication for connected environments.

Multimodal Intelligent Communication and Sensing is a research group at the School of Computer Science, Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications. Led by Dr. Yiping Zuo, the group focuses on multimodal sensing, integrated sensing and communication, and intelligent communication, with an emphasis on research platforms, interdisciplinary collaboration, and student-led exploration.

02

Supervisor

Yiping Zuo

Lecturer / Master Supervisor

School of Computer Science, Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications

Profile
Yiping Zuo is a lecturer and master supervisor at the School of Computer Science, NJUPT, and received her Ph.D. from Southeast University. She has served as a reviewer for IEEE TWC, TVT, TCOM, and TMC, led the NSFC Young Scientists Fund project and a Jiangsu higher education basic science project, and participated in an NSFC special project.
Research Interests
Multimodal sensing, wireless communication, artificial intelligence, edge computing, and integrated sensing and communication

Representative Works

01

A survey of blockchain and artificial intelligence for 6G wireless communications

Authors: Yiping Zuo, Jiajia Guo, Ning Gao, Yongxu Zhu, Shi Jin, Xiao Li

Venue: IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials, 25(4), 2494-2528 · Year: 2023

This survey reviews the integration of blockchain and artificial intelligence for 6G wireless communications, covering resource management, network architectures, privacy and security, efficiency, and cost.

View paper

02

Contractskill: Repairable contract-based skills for multimodal web agents

Authors: Zijian Lu, Yiping Zuo, Yupeng Nie, Xin He, Weibei Fan, Lianyong Qi, Shi Jin

Venue: arXiv preprint arXiv:2603.20340 · Year: 2026

This work introduces ContractSkill, a framework that converts draft web-agent skills into executable, verifiable, and locally repairable structured skills for more stable multimodal web automation.

View paper

03

MulSense: An Enhanced Multi-Person Intrusion Detection System Using Commodity WiFi Devices

Authors: Xiaoying Yang, Zishen Xu, Yiping Zuo, Weicong Chen, Jiajia Guo, Chen Dai

Venue: 2026 29th International Conference on Computer Supported Cooperative Work in Design (CSCWD), 4202-4207 · Year: 2026

MulSense targets WiFi CSI-based intrusion detection in complex multi-person scenarios, using multi-antenna transmit-receive geometry and an enhanced CSI-ratio model to improve robustness and accuracy.
